Race. Hustle. Build the baddest ride in the city.

  • Green ring = Pickup Spot. Stop in it to load your trunk for cash runs.
  • Floating diamonds = customers around the city. Pull up and stop to make the drop.

  • ⚓ Harbor Dock (east edge of the city — tap the minimap for GPS): buy a Speedboat, Cigarette Racer or Mega Yacht, hire at least 1 boat crew ($500), and sail to Isla Dorada to load up cheap from Rico. Your first run to Rico opens two more islands with even cheaper bulk loads. Pirates and the Coast Guard will try to jack your load — outrun them or shoot it out.
