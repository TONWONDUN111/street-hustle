PRIVACY POLICY

Last updated November 15, 2026. This policy covers Street Hustle Gang ("the Game") at streethustlegang.com, run by Andy McDonald / penkeymybiz.

1. What we collect

Your game save is stored on your own device (browser storage). We don't need an account, name or email to play.

Leaderboard: if you appear on the leaderboard, we store the player name you choose, your score and a random player ID.

Purchases: payments are handled by Stripe. We receive your email address (if you give it to Stripe), the pack you bought and a payment reference so we can deliver your in-game cash. We never see or store your card number. See Stripe's privacy policy.

Analytics and advertising: we may use Google Analytics, the Meta Pixel and the TikTok Pixel. These tools use cookies or similar technology to collect device and usage information (pages visited, game started, app installed, purchases) so we can measure and improve our ads. These companies process that data under their own privacy policies.

2. How we use it

To run the Game, deliver purchases, show the leaderboard, prevent fraud, and measure which ads bring players. We don't sell your personal information.

3. Your choices

You can clear your browser storage at any time (this also erases your save). You can block cookies in your browser, use ad settings on Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and Google to limit personalized ads, or opt out of Google Analytics at tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout.

Depending on where you live (for example California or Colorado), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to opt out of targeted advertising. Email us and we'll respond within 45 days.

4. Age

The Game is for players 17 and older. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

5. Retention

We keep purchase records as long as required for tax and accounting, and leaderboard entries while the leaderboard runs.

6. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
